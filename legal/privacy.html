Product Trust & Verification (“the app”) is run by [COMPANY NAME] (“we”). This policy explains what personal information the app handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It is written with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in mind.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and account type (customer or manufacturer). Manufacturers also give an organisation name and may add a company logo. If you sign in with a password, Firebase handles it; we never see or store your password. You can also sign in with Google.
  • Scan history: which product barcodes you scan, and when, linked to your account. You can also save products and ingredient checks.
  • Reports you send: the product, your reason and description, the store and area you enter, and any photos you attach.
  • Location (optional): only if you choose to show a report on the Safety Map or to see reports near you. See “Location” below.
  • Food safety preferences (optional): allergies, custom allergens and dietary preferences you choose to enter, so the app can compare them with ingredient lists. This can be health-related information. You can skip it, and you can change or delete it at any time.
  • Photos you add: evidence photos on a report, an ingredient-label photo, or a company logo.
  • Sign-in codes: if email sign-in codes are switched on, we use your email address to send you a one-time code.

Why we use it

  • To give you the app’s features: checking products, comparing ingredients with your preferences, and reporting problems.
  • To let our team review reports and, where a product belongs to a registered manufacturer, to let that manufacturer see and answer the report.
  • To keep the app secure and prevent misuse.

Who can see what

  • Our team (administrators) can see reports, photos, scan activity and account details needed to run the service.
  • A manufacturer can see reports about its own products, including your written report, the area you entered and your photos. It does not receive your name or email address, and it cannot read your exact location.
  • Other app users can see on the Safety Map that a product was reported in a general area (rounded to roughly a kilometre), the product name and its status. They do not see your name, your text or your photos.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you use a feature that needs it. If you add a location to a report, the exact point is stored privately: only you and our administrators can read it. Other people see a rounded, approximate position. You can decline, and then type an area instead.

Services we use to run the app

To provide the app we use other companies. They receive only what is needed for their task:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database): your account and the information above. Servers may be outside South Africa.
  • Open Food Facts, UPCitemdb and USDA FoodData Central: the barcode number you scan, to look up product information.
  • SerpAPI (web search): barcode numbers and product names, to find public product pages.
  • Recall check (our own free lookup service on Cloudflare, which asks the National Consumer Commission of South Africa, the US Consumer Product Safety Commission and the US Food and Drug Administration): the barcode number, product name and brand of the product you scan. It does not receive your name, email or any account detail.
  • OCR.Space: label photos or image links, to read printed label text when you choose to scan a label.
  • Cloudflare and Brevo (if email sign-in codes are on): your email address and a one-time code, to send and check the code.
  • Google Sign-In (if you choose it).

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account (see how to delete your account) your profile, scan history, saved items, and the photos and exact locations on your reports are deleted. For a manufacturer account, the company profile, logo, products, batches, serial numbers and recalls are deleted too. The written part of a report is kept without your name, so that product safety records are not lost.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. You can edit your name and preferences in the app under Settings, and you can delete your account there. Otherwise contact us using the details below. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access is restricted by account type. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children
