Customers and manufacturers: delete it in the app

  1. Open the app and go to Profile → Settings → Email & security.
  2. Tap Delete My Account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm (not needed if you signed in with Google), then tap Delete permanently.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile, including your food safety preferences.
  • Manufacturers: your company profile and logo, and all the products, batches, serial numbers and recalls you registered.
  • Your scan history, saved products and ingredient checks.
  • The photos and the exact location attached to your reports.

What is kept

  • The written part of a report you sent, with your name and account removed, so that product safety records are not lost. For manufacturers, reports about your products stay without any link to your company. Anonymous map positions (rounded to roughly a kilometre) are also kept.
