## Full description
Product Trust & Verification helps you check what you buy.

- Scan a barcode and see what is known about the product, with the source of every detail.
- We only show details we can confirm. What we cannot confirm is clearly marked, never guessed.
- Compare ingredient lists with the allergies and dietary preferences you choose to enter.
- Report suspicious, counterfeit or unsafe products, with photos and a location if you wish, and follow the outcome.
- Safety Map: see where shoppers have reported products near you. Only a general area is shown.
- Recall information appears where a manufacturer has published it.
- Manufacturers can register their products, add their company logo and respond to reports about their products.

Product Trust helps you make informed choices. It does not replace the information printed on the product or advice from a
health professional. If you have allergies, always read the label.

## Content rating (draft answers)
No violence, sexual content, gambling, or drugs. Users can submit reports (text and photos) that are reviewed by the team and, for
registered products, seen by the manufacturer; other users see only an approximate map position and a status. Location sharing is optional.
